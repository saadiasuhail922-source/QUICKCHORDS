Everything a chorus needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

A single-purpose workspace. Type on the left, read the finished sheet on the right.

i.

Chords above, never beside

Every chord is anchored to its lyric, so a long Fmaj7 can’t crash into the next one, even after you transpose.

ii.

Real chromatic transposition

Sharps and flats chosen to fit the key. Qualities survive the move: maj7, m7, sus2, sus4, add9, dim, and slash chords like G/B.

iii.

A capo helper that thinks ahead

Pick capo 1–7 and see the open shapes you’ll play next to the key that actually sounds.

iv.

Moods, then fine-tuning

Warm, late-night, uplifting or melancholic. Then click any chord to cycle it, and copy a clean plain-text sheet.

How it works

Three moves to a playable sheet.

Transpose & copy

Nudge ±1 semitone to fit your voice, set a capo, then copy a tidy chord sheet in one click.
